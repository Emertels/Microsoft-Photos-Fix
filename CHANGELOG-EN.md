## 🚀 [v1.0.0] — 2026-09-21

* **Official Release:** Advanced PowerShell automation script resolving chronic launch routing and wallpaper association bugs in Microsoft Photos on Windows 10 and 11.
* **Native C# Launcher:** Local runtime compilation of isolated launcher (`FotosModernRouteLauncherV2.exe`) with no third-party dependencies.
* **Support for 20 Image Formats:** Full associations for `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.bmp`, `.webp`, `.heic`, `.avif`, `.jxl`, `.svg`, and more.
* **Automatic Backup & Rollback:** Instant registry snapshot before modification and one-click rollback with `-Desfazer`.
* **Zero Destructive Changes:** Safe user-scope (`HKCU`) execution fully compliant with Windows Defender standards.
